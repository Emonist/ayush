Commands
Easy to learn, hard to give up.

Ayuu Music uses slash commands that are auto-completed by Discord. No complex prefixes or spam.

Command Description Category
/play <song> Search or paste a link to play music in your voice channel. Music
/skip Skip the current track. Optional vote skip in big servers. Music
/pause Pause the current track and resume anytime. Music
/queue See the upcoming tracks and manage the queue. Queue
/filter Enable filters like bassboost, nightcore, 8D and more. Filters
/24_7 Toggle 24/7 mode so the bot never leaves your channel. Utility
/volume Adjust the volume without touching Discord sliders. Utility
/help View all commands and their explanations inside Discord. Info

Help
Frequently asked questions.

If your question isn't listed here, you can always ask directly in the support server.

Is Ayuu Music free to use?

+
Yes. All core music features like play, pause, queue, filters and 24/7 mode are completely free. If premium is added in future, it will only unlock extra cosmetic features — not the basic music commands.

Why is the bot not joining my voice channel?

+
Make sure Ayuu has Connect and Speak permissions in that channel. Also check if your server has any permission overrides or a music-only voice channel enabled.

Can I restrict who can control the music?

+
Yes. You can configure a DJ role and allow only that role (or admins) to use sensitive commands like /skip, /stop or /clear. Check the in-bot /help panel for details.

Does Ayuu support Spotify or Apple Music?

+
You can paste Spotify playlist or track links and Ayuu will try to find the closest match from supported sources (for example YouTube). Direct Apple Music support depends on platform rules and may be limited.

Is the bot online 24/7?

+
The bot is hosted on reliable infrastructure with auto restart, so uptime is very high. Rare restarts may happen for updates or maintenance, but music will resume from new commands quickly.

How do I report a bug or suggest a feature?

+
Join the support server and open a ticket or post in the feedback channel with as much detail as possible (error messages, screenshots, what command you used etc.). This helps fix issues much faster.
